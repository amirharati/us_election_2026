# Low-poll coverage review — 2026-09-25

Point-in-time research audit of forecast `20260925T162252.753519Z`, cutoff **2026-09-25**. This review does not change the published forecast or import outside polling. Live coverage counts remain automatically generated in notebook 04. External source findings below require explicit source records and question-level validation before ingestion.

Reviewed all **21 states failing the recent-coverage threshold**, plus Montana. Recent means fieldwork ended within 30 days; adequate coverage requires at least three distinct samples and two firms. All-cycle samples differ from question counts: repeated questions from one survey are collapsed. A missing recent poll is not proof that the model has no polling evidence.

## Confirmed issues

1. **Idaho: missing recent survey.** The [ATR primary release](https://www.advancedtargetingresearch.com/_files/ugd/03a624_0fca9a6dd7f846f7bc96f97429561064.pdf) reports 700 registered voters, Sept 13–16, published Sept 17. Achilles is an independent. Its fuller three-candidate ballot has Achilles 45.5%, Risch 39.4%, Loesby 4.5%, unsure 10.6%. A separate two-candidate ballot gives 50.1–38.5. Neither is in our feed. These are one survey, not two independent samples. This confirms availability, not pollster accuracy; methodology and candidate coverage must be recorded before use.
2. **Virginia: missing current-matchup surveys.** TPSI's [May release](https://tpsiofficial.substack.com/p/the-virginia-project-the-public-sentiment) and [June release](https://tpsiofficial.substack.com/p/tpsi-new-tpsi-senate-poll-confirms) test Warner versus Mizusawa. June reports 50.3–32.8 with 996 likely voters. Both are absent from our feed. Their age means they would improve all-cycle coverage but still leave zero recent samples. Full field dates, question variants and sample identities need to be captured from the releases/crosstabs.
3. **Louisiana: candidate identity mismatch.** The feed assigns July PPP's “James Davis” a different UUID from September Hart's “Jamie Davis.” The [contemporaneous report](https://bayouprogressive.com/2026/08/new-poll-puts-jamie-davis-four-points-behind-julia-letlow-as-1-in-5-voters-remain-undecided/) identifies Jamie Davis and describes the initial 37–41 ballot, followed by a separate informed 38–42 ballot. The initial question should be admitted using an explicit, sourced alias. Preserve the source identity and avoid fuzzy name matching.
4. **Montana: mixed scenarios, rather than missing recent polls.** All three recent surveys—Rutgers-Eagleton (Sept 7), Aspect (Sept 15), and Quantus (Sept 15)—are eligible. The current forecast uses 12 samples across the cycle. However, `simple_baselines.prepare_inputs` averages tied questions within a sample, and the broadened independent mapping admits incomplete-field questions. Hart June24, GQR July26 and Hart Aug4 contain both full-field and head-to-head questions. The latter assume an opponent disappears and should not be averaged into the actual-field forecast. Retain them as conditional scenarios. This is a limitation introduced by the previous broadening of accepted matchups.
